### What I work on

I sit between the business and the model. Most of my time goes to turning messy requirements into something a system can actually do, then making sure the result can be explained, governed and handed over to someone else.

- Designing AI agents and assistants that do real back-office work rather than demos
- Responsible AI and data governance, the parts that survive an audit
- Business analysis and change work in regulated, ERP heavy environments
- Weekend projects in Swift, Python and TypeScript, mostly to keep my hands dirty
